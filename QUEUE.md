`nw_sim` runs a Margay firmware on a simavr ATmega1284P with the board hung off it: a DS3231 at 0x68 (time, both alarms, the INT pin), an SD card in SPI mode backed by a card image, a BME280 at 0x77, an MCP3421 at 0x69, the MCU's own ADC fed for the battery, and the 3V3 auxiliary rail switching its chips in and out. Forty simulated seconds take about 2.5 s. `tests/run.sh` diffs the boot transcript against a recorded baseline and was shown to bite.
FINDINGS: the two SD details SdFat depends on (a fill byte before a command's response; CMD12 arriving mid-stream), and that a part holding an MCU pin needs both the level (simavr's external mask, which outranks the firmware's own pullup) and the edge (the pin IRQ the external-interrupt module watches). The wake-and-log failure was my wiring, not the firmware: a Margay v2.0 and up has RTCInt on D2, not D10.
