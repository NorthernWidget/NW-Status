# Margay cold sweep: semiconductor lines against the −40 °C rule

Status: COMPLETE (2026-11-11)

Agent report, 2026-11-11. Input: `2026-11-11-margay-bom-export.csv` (exported from the KiCad schematic, 2026-11-11). Lines in scope: U1–U14, RTC1, Q1, Q2, Q3, D1/D4, D2/D3, XTAL1 (21 lines). Rule (adopted 2026-11-11): every part on a field board rated to −40 °C, AEC-Q grade where Digi-Key has one, no obsolete parts.

Evidence order: manufacturer's datasheet first, Digi-Key product page second. "Basis" says whether the −40 °C figure is tested/guaranteed, by design, or only a distributor parametric field. Anything not confirmed by a source fetched in this session is marked UNVERIFIED. DK stock is the figure on the fetch date (2026-11-11).

**Verdicts.** PASS: rated to −40 °C or colder by the maker's datasheet, Active, and no AEC-Q pin-compatible part found in stock at Digi-Key. FAIL (grade rule): passes −40 °C, but an AEC-Q pin-compatible part is in stock at Digi-Key, which the rule says to use. CHECK: passes −40 °C, with a stock or availability question. **No line fails the −40 °C floor and no line is obsolete.** The AEC-Q search covered the same part family (TI "-Q1", Diodes "Q", Maxim "/V+") for every line, and other makers only for the two discretes that have 0 stock (Q3, D1/D4). It did not look for cross-maker AEC-Q equivalents for the ICs.

**Counts (21 lines):** PASS 11, CHECK 6, FAIL 4. All 21 meet the −40 °C floor by the maker's datasheet (U2 by Digi-Key only), and none is obsolete. The 4 FAILs are the grade rule: U3, Q2, Q3 and D1/D4 have an AEC-Q pin-compatible part in stock at Digi-Key. Q3 and D1/D4 also have 0 Digi-Key stock today, as do U2, U11 and U12.
