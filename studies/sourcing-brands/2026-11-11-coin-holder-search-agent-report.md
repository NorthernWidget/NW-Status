## Materials, reported apart from the ratings

The three leading candidates are one-piece phosphor-bronze retainers with no plastic: MPD BK-916-TR ("0.25mm PHOSPHOR BRONZE, NICKEL PLATED OVERALL"), Harwin S8201-46R ("Phosphor Bronze", "Tin over Nickel") and Keystone 3000/3080 (".010 (.25) Phosphor Bronze"). Phosphor bronze is also the contact metal of the holders whose makers rate them to −40 °C or below here: MPD BC501SM (with a glass-filled PPS housing), Harwin S8411-45R (PPS housing, −55 °C), Keystone 1056 (glass-filled PPS) and 1072 (PA9T). The cell's negative face sits on a PCB pad in all three retainers, as it does on the present Linx footprint. These are statements of material only; no rating is inferred from them.

## Best options

Ranked by (1) fits the space, (2) rated −40 °C by the maker, (3) stocked at Digi-Key (all fetched 2026-11-11):
