- beginBoard (Okapi.cpp:54-125): powerAuto(); _rtc.begin/clearAlarm; _adcOb.begin(); _adcExt.begin(); _dac.begin(0x62); _dac.SetRef(BUFFERED_VREF); bme280.begin(0x77) (failure is reported, not fatal); readIdentity(); serialTimeSet(); attachLoggerInterrupts(true); I2Ctest(); clockTest(); SDtest(); enviroStats(). _io (MCP23018) is NEVER begin()'d; its only use is afterLogEvent's _io.digitalWrite(FeatherEN, HIGH, Port::B) (Okapi.cpp:451).
- powerAuto (Okapi.cpp:527-553): writes PC2/PC3 (C0/C1 select which pack feeds the bus: C1 high = V_BETA, C0 high = V_PRIME), waits 2 ms, reads PC7 as a power-good (EN_BUS_PRIME). Returns 0 (beta good), 1 (prime good), 2 (neither). Quirk: line 529 sets DDRC |= 0x0C and line 530 immediately overwrites DDRC with DDR_Prev & 0x7F, so powerAuto itself does not make C0/C1 outputs (beginBoard's pinMode at 55-56 does). Model needs: PC7 driven by a rail model as a function of PC2/PC3 and which packs are present.
- readOnBoard (Okapi.cpp:184-253): ADC_Sense_SW (PB0) high; ADS1115 0x48 single-ended channels 0, 2, then PB0 low, then channels 1, 3; _rtc.getTemp(); getTime(); then pacFind() (scan 0x10-0x1F, register 0xFD expecting 0x5B), CTRL 0x01 <- 0x10 (single-shot), REFRESH_V 0x1F (send-byte), VBUS1-4 at 0x07-0x0A two bytes MSB first, 32 V full scale.
- afterLogEvent (Okapi.cpp:447-484): only when _logCount >= _logCountPush (5) and _powerState == 0: MCP23018 GPB7 high, busy-wait 1000 ms, prints "START BACKHAUL", rows, "END BACKHAUL" on Serial (UART0, the same port as the debug transcript), then busy-waits while digitalRead(FeatherGPIO = PA5) is HIGH up to 180000 ms. FeatherEN is never driven low again in this file. With PA5 floating/low the wait ends at once; held high it costs 180 s of simulated busy time.
- sleepNow/turnOffSDcard/turnOnSDcard (Okapi.cpp:615-754): turnOffSDcard sets PC7, PC6, PC0, PC1, PD0, PD1 to inputs, Serial.end(), powerAux(OFF) (PC2/PC3 low = the bus rail OFF), SD_CS low, SPCR = 0, power_spi_disable(); SLEEP_MODE_PWR_DOWN; on wake turnOnSDcard: powerAuto() again, SPCR restore, _sd.begin(SD_CS, 8 MHz). So every sleep cycles the rail that powers (at least) the card: the model must drop and restore the card's state with the rail, or at least tolerate re-init.

## Confirmed facts, netlist side (appended as found)

- MCU pin nets (IC1, ATMEGA1284M): PB0 ~LED_EN (U45 PCA9634 ~OE, R18); PB1 SD_CD (SD1 CD); PB2 RTC_INT (RTC1 SQW); PB3 GPIO_2; PB4 SD_CS (via U25 SN74AUP3G34 buffer); PB5-7 MOSI/MISO/SCK (ISP1, SD1 MISO direct, U25 buffer, U27 to ports; MISO also to J9 B-SoM pin 50 and MS1 Feather pin 13); PC0 SCL; PC1 SDA (to U24 SN74LVC1G3157 common pin only); PC2 C0, PC3 C1 (U16 SN74AUP2G08, U40 SN74AHC1G86); PC4 ALIVE_LED; PC5 SDA_CTRL (U24 select); PC6 EN_BUS_SEC, PC7 EN_BUS_PRIME (U14/U15 SN74AUP2G08, R24/R25 to 3V3_AUX); PD0 RX / PD1 TX (UART0, to J9 pins 36/38 and MS1 pins 15/14, R38/R40); PD2 RX_EXT / PD3 TX_EXT (UART1, via U29/U42 TXS0104 to ports); PD4 GPIO_0; PD5-7 LED1-3 (R44, R42, R43); PA0 CS_EXT; PA1 GPIO_1; PA2 GPIO_3; PA3 LOG_INT (S3 button, R45); PA4 GLOBAL_INT (U41 INTA); PA5 FEATHER_GPIO (J9 pin 43 ADC5, MS1 pin 10 A5); PA6 FEATHER_CTS, PA7 FEATHER_RTS (J9 40/42, MS1 20/19). RESET is shared with U41 ~RESET, ISP, S4 and Q4 drain (something can reset the MCU through Q4).
- I2C topology: SCL (PC0) is one net to every on-board chip and to the port level shifters (U28/U36 TCA9406) and to U26/U34 TXS0102. SDA (PC1) goes only to U24 (SN74LVC1G3157, VCC = 3V3_AUX, select = PC5 SDA_CTRL); its B1 side is SDA_OB (RTC1, U31 BME280, U32 PAC1934, U35 MB85RC, U38 MLX90393, U41 MCP23018, U43 BMA456, U44 VEML6030, U45 PCA9634, U26/U34 shifters), its B2 side is SDA_EXT (U28/U36 to the ports). JP5 bridges SDA_OB and SDA_EXT. Consequence for the model: the bus switch selects SDA only, and because U24 is powered from 3V3_AUX, nothing at all on SDA is reachable while 3V3_AUX is off, including the 3V3_CORE chips (PAC1934, MCP23018, PCA9634, MB85RC).

- Rails and what switches them:
  - 3V3_CORE: U9 TPS79733 LDO (PG -> MCP23018 GPB1 PG_LDO). Always on with the logger. Powers the MCU, PAC1934, MCP23018, PCA9634, MB85RC, TPL5111, RGB D5 anode, Q3 source.
  - POWER_EN = C0 XOR C1 (U40 SN74AHC1G86 on PC2, PC3). U21 (V_PRIME -> V_HP) ON = POWER_EN AND C0; U22 (V_BETA -> V_HP) ON = POWER_EN AND C1 (U16 SN74AUP2G08). V_HP feeds U3 TPS61032 boost -> 5V_AUX (EN = POWER_EN), and 5V_AUX feeds U2 TPS62822 buck -> 3V3_AUX (EN = POWER_EN; PG -> GPB2). U13 inverts POWER_EN onto Q7 (a 5V_AUX discharge, by its D on 5V_AUX). So C1:C0 = 10 runs the aux rails from V_BETA, 01 from V_PRIME, 00 and 11 turn them off. This matches powerAux's docstring (Okapi.h:186) and powerAuto's sequence.
  - 3V3_AUX powers: SD card (SD1 VDD) and its SPI buffer U25 (MOSI, SCK, CS), RTC1 VCC (the DS3231M then runs from its coin cell BT1), BME280, MLX90393, BMA456, VEML6030, the SDA bus switch U24, the AND gates U14/U15, and the pullups R24/R25 (EN_BUS_SEC/PRIME) and R41 (SD_CD).
  - 5V_AUX powers ADS1115 U33, MCP4726 U30, the 5 V side of U26, and the Feather USB pin (MS1.26).
  - What powerAuto actually reads (Okapi.cpp:543): PC7 is made an input with PORTC bit 7 low, so it reads R25's pullup to 3V3_AUX: HIGH exactly when 3V3_AUX is up. On this board PC7 is EN_BUS_PRIME, an AND input (U15, with switch S2) that enables the prime sensor-port power switches U19/U20 (MIC2544). The firmware's "power good" read works by way of that pullup; the model gets it for free by driving PC7 from the 3V3_AUX state through a 10k pullup when the MCU does not drive it.
  - Every sleepNow() turns the aux rails off (powerAux(OFF), Okapi.cpp:707) and turnOnSDcard() brings them back with powerAuto (Okapi.cpp:736). So the clock moves to its coin cell, the BME280 loses its configuration, and the card loses power, every cycle. NW-Sim's Margay model holds the clock powered on a v3.0 (board_margay.h header comment); Okapi needs a real rail model or the same declared simplification.
- Feather/B-SoM (MS1 FeatherWing footprint and J9 B-SoM M.2 socket, in parallel):
  - UART0 PD0/PD1 is the Feather's TX/RX (J9 36/38, MS1 15/14). The firmware's debug Serial and the backhaul are the same port (Okapi.cpp:466-476 prints to Serial).
  - FEATHER_RTS PA7, FEATHER_CTS PA6, FEATHER_GPIO PA5 (Feather A5, B-SoM ADC5). RST_TRIG (Feather GPIO9, B-SoM PWM0, R54 10k to GND) drives Q4, whose drain is the MCU's RESET: the radio can reset the logger.
  - The radio's own I2C (SDA_FEATHER/SCL_FEATHER) joins SDA_OB/SCL through U34 TXS0102, enabled by 3V3_FEATHER: a powered radio sits on the on-board bus. The radio also sees the SD card's SPI (MISO, MOSI_BUF, SCK_BUF), without a chip select of its own on SD1.
  - Power: FEATHER_BAT = U10 TPS22917 output from V_LI, ON = TPL5111 DRVn (U8.5). TPL5111: VDD 3V3_CORE (pin 1), EN/ONE_SHOT 3V3_CORE (pin 6), DELAY/M_DRV (pin 3) = R20 130k to GND and Q3 drain; Q3 FDN360P source 3V3_CORE, gate FEATHER_EN (MCP23018 GPB7) with R19 100k to 3V3_CORE. So FEATHER_EN LOW turns Q3 on and pulls M_DRV to VDD (the TPL5111 manual power-on); HIGH, which is what afterLogEvent writes "to turn on Feather power" (Okapi.cpp:451), turns it off. DONE = WD_DONE from the radio (Feather A3, B-SoM ADC3). R62 and JP8, named in the Okapi.cpp:34-35 comment, do not appear in this KiCad netlist: here R20 130k is fitted directly.
- LEDs: RGB D5 (CLVBA) cathodes red R44/PD5, blue R42/PD6, green R43/PD7 (same pins and colors as the Margay model's red/blue/green). ALIVE_LED PC4 -> R37 330 -> D4 green cathode (D4 is a second CLVBA on the USB_ISP_Feather sheet). The 8-LED grid is U45 PCA9634 (D10-D14, D16-D18) and is not driven by any firmware: no PCA9634 code exists in Okapi_Library. PB0 (~LED_EN, R18 100k pullup to 3V3_CORE) is what the firmware calls ADC_Sense_SW and toggles in readOnBoard (Okapi.cpp:208-217), leaving it LOW, which enables the PCA9634 outputs.

- **Consequence for the firmware, from the MCP23018 page**: Okapi_Library never calls _io.begin() or _io.pinMode() (grep of Okapi.cpp: `_io` appears at lines 7 and 451 only). The only write is OLATB bit 7 (MCP23018.cpp:59-65 writes REG_OLAT + port). With IODIRB still 0xFF from power-up, GPB7 stays an input, R19 holds FEATHER_EN high and Q3 off: the backhaul's write changes nothing on the wire, and the radio is powered only by the TPL5111's own 66-minute timer. A datasheet-built MCP23018 model shows this without being told.
- **Consequence from the VEML6030 page**: on this board the firmware's _adcOb (ADS1115 protocol at 0x48) talks to a light sensor. I2Ctest() passes 0x48 (the VEML6030 ACKs), so the phantom ADC is invisible at boot; readADC_SingleEnded writes two bytes to VEML6030 command 0x01 (ALS_WH, the high threshold window) and reads command 0x00 (ALS_CONF) after a STOP (Adafruit_ADS1015.cpp:98-102 ends the pointer write with endTransmission()), where the VEML6030's documented read uses a repeated start. Whether a VEML6030 honors a command code across a STOP is not stated on p.6: an open question for the model.

## The hard parts

1. **The radio handshake.** The Boron is an nRF52840: simavr cannot run it and should not try. The link is fully observable at the board's pins (UART0, PA5, RST_TRIG, WD_DONE, FEATHER_BAT), and the radio's own I2C on U34 is idle in the only sketch that exists. A scripted C peer in `src/parts/` is the right model, with its behavior taken from Boron_Test.ino and labeled as a sketch model, not a datasheet part. Needs one new simavr hookup NW-Sim does not use yet: feeding bytes into UART0 RX (`UART_IRQ_INPUT`, with the XON/XOFF flow-control IRQs nw_sim.c already handles for output). Its transcript must separate what the radio heard from the logger's console, though both are the same wire.
2. **Reset from outside.** RST_TRIG -> Q4 -> RESET resets the 1284P. simavr's `avr_reset()` preserves reset flags set beforehand (sim_avr.c:214-241), so the board sets EXTRF in MCUSR then calls it. This is what lets the B7(a) MCUSR item and the TPL5010 (#35, not yet in the netlist) be tested at all. Needs a conformance check that MCUSR reads EXTRF after the pulse and PORF only at power-on.
3. **TPL5111 and TPL5010.** The TPL5111 is a timer on the radio's power, easy (S). The TPL5010 watchdog of DECISIONS item 2 is not on the KiCad netlist yet: model it when its net lands, from Margay's plan; it is the same part a Margay model will want.
4. **Rails.** The aux rails switch on every sleep. Margay's model holds the clock powered and treats the card as always powered; Okapi's firmware cuts 3V3_AUX each cycle, so the card, the BME280 and the clock's VCC go with it. Recommend modeling it for real (S-M on top of the board file), reusing the DS3231 VCC/VBAT work in progress. The analog rail voltages are not needed: a rail is on or off, and the scripted pack voltages feed the PAC1934.
5. **The LED grid.** Model the PCA9634 and its trace now, but there is no firmware to test it against; its first case waits for the driver (Okapi_Library#11 references). ~OE on PB0 is driven LOW by readOnBoard today, so the trace must include OE.
6. **simavr itself.** Nothing missing for the first milestone (measured: the firmware runs through boot, sleep, wake and the card on the Margay proxy). Possible gaps later: none identified; multi-master I2C is not needed while the radio stays off the bus.

## Risks
