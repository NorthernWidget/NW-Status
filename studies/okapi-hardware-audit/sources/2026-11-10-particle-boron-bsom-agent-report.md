### Netlist facts found during this study (a29ca5e, regenerated today)

- NL1. SPI is also wired to both sockets, a set the v0.8 plan's signal table omits: net MISO (IC1 PB6, ISP1, SD1 DATA_OUT, U27 A2, MS1 13, J9 50); MOSI_BUF (U25 1Y, SD1 DATA_IN, MS1 12, J9 52); SCK_BUF (U25 2Y, SD1 SCLK, MS1 11, J9 54). The Particle's SPI pins sit on the SD card bus. These are three more lines that can drive an unpowered module (plan problem 6), and a Particle sketch must leave MISO/MOSI/SCK as inputs (Device OS default for an unused pin is UNVERIFIED) or it fights the SD card.
- NL2. MS1 1 RESET, MS1 3 AREF (Boron MODE) and MS1 27 EN are unconnected. J9 34 RESET and 32 MODE have buttons and pull-ups (plan table).

### How Particle's own carrier boards power a B-SoM

- EV1. B-Series evaluation board, https://docs.particle.io/reference/datasheets/b-series/b-series-eval-board/ ("Powering the board"): "The B524/B523 require a LiPo battery when powering by USB due to the higher current requirements of 2G/3G. The B404X/B404/B402 (LTE Cat M1) can be powered by USB without a battery." Minimum 5 V at 500 mA with the LiPo, 5 V at 2000 mA without. "Power jumpers": J5 SOM_VCC, J31 SOM_3V3. "Description" item 6: "SoM power switch: Controls 3V3 power to the SoM".
- EV2. Eval board schematic, https://github.com/particle-iot/som-eval-board, eagle/v1.1/SOM EVAL BOARD V1.1.sch (master), nets traced from the Eagle XML with a script: bq24195 U14 SYS -> net PMIC_SYS (10 uF + 10 uF + 1 nF) -> U2 XC8107 load switch (CE = SYS_PWR_EN, R4 100 k pull-up to PMIC_SYS, switch S1) -> net VCC (C4 10 uF, C6 0.1 uF) -> jumper J5 -> UVCC = M.2 pins 2, 4, 6, 8. The same switched VCC feeds U3 XC9258A (3.3 V buck, CE via R11 10 k to VCC) -> +3V3 -> jumper J31 -> M.2 pins 10, 12. M.2 pin 16 (VUSB) sits on USB1 VBUS through F1 (6 V 1 A) and R61 (0.001 ohm shunt), and that node feeds the PMIC input through Schottky D4; the SoM gets 5 V on pin 16 for USB detection, and the board takes its own power from the same USB node. The same architecture as the Boron (BR5): one switch after the PMIC gates both the modem and the 3.3 V buck, and 3.3 V can only rise after VCC.
- EV3. PM-BAT power module (used on the M.2 SoM breakout), https://docs.particle.io/hardware/power/pm-bat-datasheet/, "Overview": "3.3V regulated output for MCU (2A)"; "3.9V output for cellular modem (2A)"; bq24195 PMIC; MAX17043 fuel gauge. "Pinout": pin 25 ENABLE "Has internal pull-up to VSYS, pull to GND to disable power outputs"; pin 24 PG "pulled low on failure of either regulator". "Schematic" (pm-bat-schematic.svg, PM-BAT v0.4, rendered and read): VSYS -> U2 TPS63020 buck-boost -> VCC (C102 220 uF 6 V + 22 uF + 0.1 uF + 100 pF + 1 nF); VSYS -> U3 TPS631011 buck-boost -> 3V3 (220 uF + 22 uF + ...); 3V3 -> U5 TPS22918 -> 3V3_AUX. PM-DC (https://docs.particle.io/hardware/power/pm-dc-datasheet/): 3.3 V at 1 A and VCC 3.9 V 2 A. Particle's current carrier design therefore regulates VCC at 3.9 V with a buck-boost rather than putting the cell straight on VCC.
- EV4. M.2 breakout, https://docs.particle.io/reference/datasheets/m-series/m2-breakout-datasheet/, "PM-BAT interface": PMIC and fuel gauge on D0/D1 and FUEL_INT on A6 (pin 45). "Install a power module": with PM-BAT "You cannot power by USB only."
- BR11. Boron power schematic, https://docs.particle.io/reference/datasheets/b-series/boron-datasheet/ "Schematic > Power" (image /assets/images/boron/schematic-power-large.png, read): bq24195L U14; BAT (pins 13, 14) on the LiPo connector J3 (the Li+ pin); SYS -> PMIC_SYS -> U2 XC8107 "POWER SWITCH" (CE = SYS_PWR_EN, R4 100 k to PMIC_SYS; this is the EN pin) -> VSYS (modem, 100 nF + 10 uF) -> U1 XC9258A33 "3.3V REGULATOR" (CE via R1 10 k to VSYS) -> +3V3 (100 nF + 10 uF). The BRN404X datasheet revision 018 (2023-03-29) says "Updated 3.3V regulator", so the current part may differ; the topology is what matters. "Schematic > Fuel gauge": MAX1704X on P0.24/SDA and P1.09/SCL, interrupt P0.05/BAT_INT; the PMIC's SDA/SCL in the power schematic are the same P0.24/P1.09. Wire (D0/D1) is P0.26/P0.27 (BR8), so on a Boron the PMIC and fuel gauge sit on a separate internal bus, not on the Okapi-facing I2C pins.

### A2. One power design (proposal)

1. Keep U10 as the only on/off for both modules. The Boron's EN is a worse off switch: it leaves 70 uA flowing (BR6), its pull-up rides PMIC_SYS (3.8 V) so it needs an open-drain driver (BR5), and it does nothing for a B-SoM.
2. Do not connect MS1 pin 2 (Boron 3V) to PARTICLE_3V3. Fit one 3.3 V regulator from PARTICLE_VCC to PARTICLE_3V3 on every board. On a B-SoM build it powers J9 pins 10, 12; on a Boron build the Boron makes its own 3.3 V and the board regulator powers only U34's VB/EN, the pull-ups, D7 and C37 (and N6's PG sense). This removes the jumper of N3 and the back-drive question entirely: no regulator output ever meets the Boron's buck output. Cost: the regulator's quiescent current while PARTICLE_VCC is on, nothing while U10 is off. This changes N3 and is Andy's call.
3. VCC floor (N7): Particle's own current power module regulates VCC to 3.9 V with a TPS63020 buck-boost (EV3), and every B-SoM datasheet gives 3.6 V as the VCC minimum. A buck-boost after U10 holding PARTICLE_VCC at about 3.9 V serves both modules (3.9 V is inside the Boron's Li+ range) and lets the modem run on the bottom of the cell. Then the 3.3 V regulator sees a fixed 3.9 V and a 500 mA LDO (TLV755P, 238 mV max dropout, RG4) has 600 mV headroom. Without the buck-boost, the LDO's dropout still holds 3.3 V down to about 3.54 V at 500 mA, below the modem's own 3.6 V floor, so the LDO is not the limit. Candidates: TPS63020 (Particle's choice) or TPS63802 for VCC; TLV755P or TLV757P (LDO) or TPS631011 (buck-boost, Particle's choice) for 3.3 V.
4. Bulk capacitance on PARTICLE_VCC: Particle's PM-BAT carries 220 uF + 22 uF on VCC and 220 uF on its input (EV3); the eval board has only 10 uF + 0.1 uF after its load switch (EV2). The B524/B504e datasheets ask for "additional bulk capacitors". A value is a proposal for Andy and couples to N4 (U10's CT).
5. Power-up order: no B-SoM document read here states a required VCC/3V3 order. Both Particle carriers derive 3.3 V from the switched VCC, so 3.3 V rises after VCC (EV2, BR11). Item 2 reproduces that order. The M-SoM adds a supervisory-reset requirement for slow 3V3 ramps (SU1).

### A3. One interface: one sketch for both?

Yes for the Boron, B404X and B524 (one source; separate binaries per platform). Every logger-facing line has the same Device OS name on both sockets: Serial1 TX/RX (D9/D10), RTS D2, CTS D3, PARTICLE_GPIO A5/D14, WD_DONE A3/D16, RST_TRIG D4, I2C D0/D1 (Wire), SPI SCK/MOSI/MISO (D13/D12/D11) (BX10, BR8, FP2, RP2). The committed Boron_Test.ino (D4, A3, D14, Serial1) would run on a B-SoM unchanged in its pin use. Exceptions: (1) A3/A5 lose PWM on the B-SoM (irrelevant to Okapi's use); (2) on an M-SoM, J9 pin 43 (A5, PARTICLE_GPIO) is SWCLK with a boot-time 40 k pull-down (SU1); (3) RTS/CTS map to swapped nRF pins (P1.01/P1.02) between Boron and B-SoM, invisible to Device OS; (4) a B-SoM sketch must leave PMIC_DETECTION off (no PMIC on Okapi, and D0/D1 carry the logger's I2C) (BX12); (5) all GPIO are 3.3 V only and must not see a driven line while unpowered (BX5, BR5).

### A4. Lifecycle facts that bear on v0.8

BRN404XKIT and BRN404XTRAY50 are GA and the BRN404X is not in Particle's deprecation table (BR9, LC1), but the generated comparison table in the Axion datasheet marks "Boron 404X" Deprecated (SU3). B404XMTY, B524MTY and B504EMTY are GA. Particle's new Feather module (Axion, Cat 1 bis, global) is "In development" with the same Feather pin names and a 500 mA 3V3 regulator. The M-SoM (M404, M524) fits J9 by name but needs 500 mA on 3V3 and has a boot-strap on pin 43. Particle's documented AVR-update path (Asset OTA) needs Device OS 5.5.0 or later.
