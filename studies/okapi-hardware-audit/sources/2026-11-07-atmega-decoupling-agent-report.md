# ATmega1284P VCC/AVCC/AREF decoupling on Okapi

## Confirmed facts

- F1 (Okapi_Library/src, grep analogRead|analogReference|ADMUX|ADCSRA|REFS): no live analogRead or analogReference call; the only analogRead lines are commented out (Okapi.cpp:186,192,193). All live analog measurement goes through two ADS1115 (_adcOb, _adcExt; Okapi.cpp:215-223, 522). ADC is disabled before sleep (Okapi.cpp:656 cbi ADEN) and re-enabled after (Okapi.cpp:670 `ADCSRA = 135;  //DEBUG!` = ADEN + prescaler/128), never converts. ADMUX is never written, so it stays at its reset value 0 (REFS=00, AREF pin) unless a user sketch calls analogRead.
- F2 Issues #15/#17/#28 read (gh issue view, 2026-11-07). Bodies say 5V_AUX; Andy's 2026-11-06 comments correct to 3V3_CORE. #15 body claims driving AREF while internal ref selected "will damage" — to check against datasheet.
- F3 (netlist q7/okapi.xml, kicadxml export of 2026-11-06 19:53): IC1 = ATMEGA1284P-MUR, footprint nw-cold-silicon:MLF44-TH (QFN/MLF-44 with exposed pad "TH" on GND). 3V3_CORE pins: VCC 5, 17, 38; AVCC 27; AREF 29. GND pins 6, 18, 28, 39 + EP. 3V3_CORE has 45 nodes incl. C18, C35, C42, C50.
- F4 (same netlist): all eight ADC-capable pins PA0-PA7 are wired as digital signals: PA0 CS_EXT, PA1 GPIO_1, PA2 GPIO_3, PA3 LOG_INT, PA4 GLOBAL_INT, PA5 FEATHER_GPIO, PA6 FEATHER_CTS, PA7 FEATHER_RTS. No analog signal reaches the internal ADC on this board (FEATHER_GPIO to J9/MS1 is the only one a user could in principle drive with an analog voltage).
- F5 (netlist): C42 1uF C0402 Samsung CL05A105KA5NQNC; C50 0.1uF C0402 Murata GCM155R71C104KA55D; C18 4.7uF C0603 GRM188Z71C475KE21D; U9 TPS79733DCKR.
- F6 (pcbnew 10.0.8 on Okapi.kicad_pcb, scripts/geom*.py): 2 copper layers. IC1 at (149.146, 104.940) F.Cu, rot 0; EP "TH" 5.0x5.0 mm SMD pad on GND with NO vias in it. Pads (centre): VCC5 (145.821,104.44) W side; VCC17 (149.146,108.265) S side; VCC38 (149.646,101.615) N side; AVCC27 (152.471,105.44) and AREF29 (152.471,104.44) E side, GND28 between them; GND6/18/39 adjacent to each VCC pin.
- F7 (same): C42 pads: 3V3_CORE (155.257,104.94), GND (153.957,104.94). Centre-to-centre from AVCC27 / AREF29 pad to C42's 3V3 pad = 2.83 mm (each; edge-to-edge ~2.1 mm, matching the audit's 2.2 mm). AVCC and AREF each reach C42 by its own 0.305 mm F.Cu track; C42 GND pad is tracked straight to GND28 (1.5 mm) and on into the EP.
- F8 (same): nearest capacitor to each VCC pad, centre-to-centre: VCC5 C42 9.45 mm, C50 33.9 mm; VCC17 C42 6.96, C50 32.9; VCC38 C42 6.52, C50 29.3. C50 (177.09,89.83) is 28-34 mm away; C35 (0.1uF) 35-42 mm; C18 (4.7uF, U9 output) 42-48 mm. Audit's "nothing within 6 mm, C50 ~32 mm" confirmed.
- F9 (same): VCC5 reaches 3V3_CORE through a via at (144.81,104.41); VCC17 via (149.15,109.26); VCC38 via (149.49,100.03); all on 0.305 mm tracks, with B.Cu 0.305 mm tracks joining them around the package. Only one GND via within 6 mm of IC1 centre: (149.50,109.96), 5.0 mm, reached from GND18. No GND pour anywhere near IC1 on either layer (KiCad zones and the Eagle Okapi.brd GND polygons are all far from IC1; Eagle IC1 at (33.02,67.56), its GND polygons span x 23-55, y 17-45).
- D1 Microchip DS40002070B (ATmega164A/PA/324A/PA/644A/PA/1284/P datasheet, 2020), §2.3.10 p.16: "AVCC is the supply voltage pin for Port A and the Analog-to-digital Converter. It should be externally connected to VCC, even if the ADC is not used. If the ADC is used, it should be connected to VCC through a low-pass filter."
- D2 DS40002070B §23.2 p.243: "AVCC must not differ more than ±0.3V from VCC." and "The voltage reference may be externally decoupled at the AREF pin by a capacitor for better noise performance."
- D3 DS40002070B §23.6.2 p.249-250: "...the external AREF pin is directly connected to the ADC, and the reference voltage can be made more immune to noise by connecting a capacitor between the AREF pin and ground. ... Note that VREF is a high impedant source, and only a capacitive load should be connected in a system. If the user has a fixed voltage source connected to the AREF pin, the user may not use the other reference voltage options in the application, as they will be shorted to the external voltage."
- D4 DS40002070B §23.9.1 Table 23-3 p.~256: REFS 00 = "AREF, Internal Vref turned off"; 01 = "AVCC with external capacitor at AREF pin"; 10 = "Internal 1.1V Voltage Reference with external capacitor at AREF pin"; 11 = "Internal 2.56V ...". Text: "The internal voltage reference options may not be used if an external reference voltage is being applied to the AREF pin." (Issue #15's REFS table, "10 reserved, 11 = 1.1 V", is wrong.) ADMUX initial value 0x00.
- D5 DS40002070B §23.7.2 p.251 + Figure 23-9 p.252 (rendered fig-252.png): "The AVCC pin on the device should be connected to the digital VCC supply voltage via an LC network as shown in Figure 23-9." Figure: 10 µH series VCC->AVCC, 100 nF AVCC->GND, inside an "Analog Ground Plane" outline around Port A/AREF/GND/AVCC; AREF drawn with nothing attached. Item 1: "Make sure analog tracks run over the analog ground plane". Item 4: ADC port pins used as digital outputs must not switch during a conversion.
- D6 DS40002070B: no grep hit for a per-VCC-pin decoupling value ("decoupl", "100 ?nF" only in Fig 23-9). The datasheet itself does not specify VCC-pin decoupling; that is in AN2519.
- D7 DS40002070B §2 note p.~20 (line 530 of text): "The large center pad underneath the VQFN/QFN/MLF package should be soldered to ground on the board to ensure good mechanical stability."
- D8 DS40002070B Table 28-17 p.338: AVCC VCC-0.3 to VCC+0.3; VREF 1.0 to AVCC; VINT1 1.0/1.1/1.2 V; VINT2 2.33/2.56/2.79 V "VCC > 2.7V"; RREF Reference Input Resistance 32 kΩ typ.
- A1 Microchip AN2519 (DS00002519B, 2018) §2.1 p.5-6: "Such a current spike cannot be delivered over long power supply lines; the main source should be the decoupling capacitor." Fig 2-1 "Incorrect Decoupling": "The capacitor is placed too far away from the microcontroller, creating a larger high-current loop." "... it is critical to minimize the loop area formed between the decoupling capacitor and device power and ground pin." "For devices with multiple pairs of power and ground pins, it is essential that there is a decoupling capacitor for every pair of pins." "The main power supply should also have a tantalum or ceramic capacitor to stabilize it." Ferrite bead in series is optional: "may not be necessary, especially if the power supply itself is sufficiently filtered."
- A2 AN2519 §2.2 p.6: "To improve the accuracy of the ADC, the analog supply voltage must be decoupled separately, similar to the digital supply voltage. AREF must also be decoupled. The typical value of the capacitor is 100 nF. If a separate analog ground (AGND) is present, the analog ground should be separated from the digital ground so that the analog and digital grounds are connected only at a single point (at the power supply GND)."
- A3 AN2519 §7 p.19 key points: "(2) ... recommended to have a multilayer design with supply and ground plane on separate layers. (3) Decoupling of all digital supply pairs from VCC and isolating AVCC from VCC. ... (5) ... the vias connected to the planes are close to the MCU pins". Figure 7-4 (ATmega324PB-MUR, extracted an-002.png): VCC pin 5 has 100n (C2) + 4.7u (C3); AVCC pin 27 fed from 3V3 through L1 "INDUCTOR" (no value) with 100n (C9) to GND; AREF/PE4 pin 29 drawn with nothing on it. (324PB has one VCC pin; pins 17/38 are PE2/PE3, so this is not a 1284P per-pin drawing.)
- B1 Okapi BoM (Hardware/Okapi_BoM.csv + netlist): inductors on board are only power inductors L1 6.8 µH 1008, L2 10 µH WE-XHMI, L3 1 µH WE-LQS, L4 4.7 µH WE-MAIA; no ferrite bead and no small signal inductor line exists.
- P1 (scripts/free.py, free_F.py; 0402 land as C42's, 0.2 mm copper clearance, courtyards respected, 0.2 mm grid search within 4 mm of each VCC pad): top side (F.Cu) has no free 0402 site near VCC5 or VCC17 without rerouting; nearest top-side site for VCC38 is about 2.6 mm off, at (150.05, 98.61) rot 0. Bottom side (B.Cu) is free directly under/beside the pins: VCC5 at (145.42-145.62, 104.84) rot 90; VCC38 at (148.65, 101.61) rot 180; VCC17 nearest at (149.55, 105.66) rot 0 (inside the EP footprint, ~2.2 mm) or (147.95, 111.26) rot 90. Bottom side currently carries only JP1-JP7 and LOGO1, no assembled parts.
- C1 LC corner (computed): 10 uH with 100 nF -> 159.2 kHz; with 1 uF -> 50.3 kHz.
- X1 Issue #15's quote "...not recommended to use the AREF pin for any other purpose" and its "will damage" claim: neither found in DS40002070B or AN2519 (grep). The datasheet's wording is "shorted to the external voltage" / "may not be used".
