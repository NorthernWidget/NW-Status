## Board check against the datasheets

Source: netlist exported with kicad-cli from Project-Okapi/Hardware/KiCAD-10import/Okapi.kicad_sch at commit dfd0f57 into net/okapi.xml (scratch). Rail-level only: the netlist cannot say which capacitor is physically next to which pin.

1. U30 MCP4726 VOUT, C33 = 0.1 µF (GCM155R71C104KA55D) alone on DAC_OUT: 1000 × the 100 pF stated in §4.4.6 "to meet electrical specifications". The phase-margin spec (66° typ) is given only at CL = 400 pF. The datasheet's own Fig 8-2 shows "C3 ~0.1 µF, Optional to reduce noise in VOUT pin", so the manufacturer contradicts itself. No RISO is present. Outside the stated load spec; stability/settling at 0.1 µF is not guaranteed by any spec line and needs a bench check or an RISO.
2. U37 TLV3691 VCC (net Net-(D8-C), fed by BAT54C D8 from 5V_LP/V_PRIME): NO capacitor on the net. Datasheet asks for a 0.1 µF ceramic at VCC.
3. U5 BQ2980 VDD filter: R9 = 330 Ω exceeds Table 8-1 RVDD MAX 300 Ω; C11 = 1 µF sits exactly at CVDD MAX 1 µF (TMK107B7105KA, 25 V, 0603; its DC-bias derating at ~4 V brings it below 1 µF effective, so the capacitor itself is within bounds).
4. U5 BQ2980 CTR: R14 100 Ω (matches RCTR) from MCP23018 GPA1 (BMS_CTR), R12 1 kΩ to GND, C12 0.1 µF to GND. τ = 100 µs. MCP23018 outputs are open-drain, so nothing on this board drives CTR HIGH against 1 kΩ (MCP23018 internal pull-up D070 IPU = 220 µA typ at VDD 5 V, which gives about 0.2 V across 1 kΩ). Not a capacitor-limit violation, but the RC bears no relation to the datasheet's 5 MΩ/1 µF hold examples – designer should confirm the intended CTR function. Also C12 loads GPA1 through 100 Ω, far above MCP23018 D101 CIO 50 pF max (characterized output-load spec).
5. U21 TPS22917 VIN (V_PRIME): only C16 0.22 µF on the net (after the 20 mΩ sense R73). Datasheet: CIN 1 µF "sufficient in most situations" (a recommendation, not a minimum).
6. U21/U22 TPS22917 into V_HP (C2 22 µF) with CT open: computed inrush = CL × SRON(typ) = 22 µF × 44 mV/µs = 0.97 A at VIN 3.6 V; 22 µF × 72 mV/µs = 1.58 A at 5.0 V; rise time 55–65 µs. Below IPLS 2.5 A (pulse < 300 µs) and IMAX 2 A, using typical slew rates (no max SR is specified). Not a violation.
7. Level shifters' B side: U27/U29 (TXS0104E VB) and U28 (TCA9406 VCCB) on VBUS_PRIME have only C20 22 µF (0805); U42/U36 on VBUS_SEC only C19 22 µF. TI's examples show 0.1 µF at each VCCB. Not a stated numeric requirement.
8. Shared-rail counts (each datasheet asks for ~0.1 µF per pin): 3V3_AUX has 6 × 0.1 µF (+2 × 22 µF, 68 pF) for 16 IC power pins (RTC1, SD1, U11, U12, U14, U15, U24, U25, U31 ×2, U38 ×2, U43 ×3, U44). 3V3_CORE has 2 × 0.1 µF + 1 µF + 4.7 µF for 12 power pins (U13, U16, U32 ×2, U34 VA, U35, U39 VIN, U40, U41, U45, U8, regulator U9 VOUT). 3V3_CORE_ISO has 5 × 0.1 µF for 7 pins. Whether each chip has a local cap is a layout question.
9. RTC1 DS3231M VCC sits on 3V3_AUX with 44 µF bulk; the datasheet's 0.1–1.0 µF range is for the pin's own decoupling, so bulk on the rail is not obviously covered by it – designer's interpretation. VBAT C34 0.1 µF (backup use: cap "not required").
10. D6 LM4040 cathode C31 0.1 µF (GCM155R71C104KA55D, 16 V X7R, at 2.5 V): > 10 nF, outside the 100 pF–10 nF unstable band. OK.
11. U30 MCP4726 VREF = same node, C31 0.1 µF (datasheet optional 0.1 µF + 10 µF). U23 FT231X: VCC C21 0.1 µF + C22 4.7 µF; 3V3OUT/VCCIO/RESET# tied with C25 0.1 µF (matches Fig 6.1); USB D± have 12 pF (C23/C24, C0G) where FTDI's figure shows 47 pF.
12. U7 LTC4412: VIN C14 10 µF (top of 0.1–10 µF range); SENSE/load C3 10 µF (within 1–47 µF typical). U10 TPS22917 VOUT (FEATHER_BAT) has no capacitor on Okapi (datasheet states no minimum).
