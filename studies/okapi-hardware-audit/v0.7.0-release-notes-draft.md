**Do not build this release.** The 2024 design's solar charger has no current-sense resistor: the LT3652's SENSE and BAT pins share one net, so it would charge the LiPo at its switch's current limit and never end the charge (#32). v0.8.0 adds the resistor. v0.7.0 is the faithful conversion, the reference that v0.8.0 is measured against.

The 2024 Okapi design (v0.6.0), converted from Eagle to KiCad. The schematic and the board agree, and the board is the 2024 Eagle board with the corrections listed below. The design is in `Hardware/KiCAD-10import/`, and `Production/` holds the fabrication files it generates.

**Checked against the Eagle originals and the 2024 fabrication files** (`Hardware/Okapi.sch`, `Hardware/Okapi.brd`, `Hardware/Okapi_2024-11-02/`):
* 204 of the Eagle schematic's 209 nets come through exactly. The other five are JP8 and R62, removed (#34), and BT1's two + springs, one terminal in KiCad.
* The copper matched the 2024 Gerbers to within 0.05 mm² before any part changed, and every later difference traces to a commit (re-audit of 2026-11-13). The ten vias that the import shrank to 0.40 mm are back at the 0.51 and 0.55 mm of the 2024 Gerbers.
* Placement: all 235 placed parts within 0.011 mm of the 2024 pick-and-place, at the same rotation. Drill hits: the same sizes, with the micro-USB shell's six holes now its three slots.
* The silkscreen prints what the 2024 board printed, including the A1 and A3 divider diagrams and the package outlines, with five changes:
  (1) the reference designators are hidden, as on Margay;
  (2) the silkscreen is clipped at the solder mask, as a fab clips it;
  (3) the stale J6, J8 and J9 labels are gone;
  (4) the LiPo connector J8 has + and − marks on the bottom and a + on the top;
  (5) IC1 has a pin-1 dot outside the package, where it prints.
* KiCad's checks: ERC 5 (two load switches feeding one rail, by design), DRC 0, schematic-to-board parity 0. The design rules are Eagle's own (6 mil to vias and through-hole pads, 5 mil elsewhere, 5 mil via ring).

**Changed from the 2024 Eagle files** (each with its issue):
* JP8 and R62 are gone; the TPL5111 wakes the telemetry module every 66 minutes from R20 (#34).
* R1, R5, R7 and R8 are 0402, and C1 and C22 0603; R22, R48 and R69–R71 sit on dual 0402/0603 lands, so a user can swap them (#33). R48 is 1 kΩ, so VREF supplies up to 2 mA (#36).
* The PAC1934's shunts have values (R72, R73, R75 20 mΩ; R74 50 mΩ) and its address is set (R51 0 Ω, 0x10) (#21).
* The USB-C shell is grounded, and both USB connectors' shell stakes sit in slots, as the 2024 files milled them.
* Every resistor line carries a second source.

**Not in this release.** Parts that would appear only in the schematic wait for the v0.8.0 layout, which places them: AREF and VCC bypass capacitors (#15, #17), the charger's sense resistor (#32), and pull resistors on SLOW, the bus-voltage switches and C0/C1 (#37, #53, #54). Until #53 lands, move the sensor-bus voltage switches only with the logger off.

**Untested.** No board has been built from these KiCad files or from the 2024 Eagle files. The KiCad project takes its library and title block from [NW-Cold-Silicon](https://github.com/NorthernWidget/NW-Cold-Silicon), cloned beside this repository; the schematic and board carry copies of every symbol and footprint, so they open without it.
